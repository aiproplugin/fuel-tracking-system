Operator Guide

Recording fuel, the right way

This guide walks you through everything you do as a fuel operator: signing in, scanning a vehicle, and recording how much fuel you dispensed — plus what to do when the app stops you with a warning.

Your one job, in a sentence When you fill a vehicle, you record how many litres you dispensed and the vehicle's current meter reading — the odometer (km) for road vehicles, the hour meter (hrs) for machines like forklifts, or the energy meter (kWh) for generators. The form always tells you which one. The app takes care of the stock maths, the efficiency, and the paperwork.

How the app is built for you

  • It works on your phone or tablet — one big action per screen.
  • You are bound to one tank. You never pick a tank; the app already knows which one is yours.
  • It stops you before a wrong entry becomes a problem — for example, if a petrol vehicle rolls up to a diesel tank.
  • Every issue is saved to a permanent record the moment you submit. There is nothing else to file.

Step 1

Signing in

Open the app in your phone's browser using the address your administrator gave you (something like https://fuel.yourcompany.internal). Add it to your home screen so it is one tap away.

  1. Enter your username The one your administrator created for you.
  2. Enter your password Then tap Sign in.
  3. You land on your home screen Showing your assigned tank and today's totals.
📷Screenshot placeholder
The login screen — logo, Username field, Password field, and the "Sign in" button.
If it says "Invalid username or password" The message is deliberately the same whether the username or the password was wrong — this protects accounts. Check both and try again. After 5 wrong tries the account locks for 15 minutes (and longer if it keeps happening). See Quick reference or ask your administrator to unlock it.

Step 2

Setting your own password

The first time you sign in — and any time an administrator resets your password — the app gives you a temporary password and immediately asks you to choose your own. You cannot use the app until you do.

  1. You land on "Set a new password" The app tells you your password was set by an administrator.
  2. Type your current (temporary) password The one you were just given.
  3. Choose a new password, then confirm it It must meet the rules below.
  4. Tap "Update password" The app signs you out so you can sign back in with your new password. This is normal.
Password rules At least 12 characters, with an upper-case letter, a lower-case letter, a digit, and a symbol (like ! or #). Pick something only you know — never share it.

Step 3

Your home screen

This is where every fuelling job starts. From top to bottom:

Assigned tank

A dark card shows the tank you are bound to, its fuel type (Petrol or Diesel), and the current stock in litres. You cannot change this — only an administrator can reassign your tank.

Scan vehicle QR

The big green button. Tap it to start a fuel issue.

Today's totals

Three small cards: Issues (how many fills today), Litres (total dispensed today), and Alerts (fills that were flagged for unusual fuel economy).

Recent issues

Your latest fills, each with the plate, litres, and time. A km/L tag (or hrs/L / kWh/L for machines and generators) means that fill was flagged as abnormal.

"No tank assigned"? If you see an amber "No tank assigned" message instead of the dark tank card, you cannot issue fuel yet. Contact your administrator — they need to bind a tank to your account. The change takes effect the next time you sign in.

Step 4

Scanning a vehicle

Every vehicle has a printed QR sticker. Scanning it is how the app knows which vehicle you are about to fuel — it never asks you to type a plate.

Scanning with the camera

  1. Tap "Scan vehicle QR" on the home screen The camera view opens.
  2. Hold the vehicle's QR code inside the frame Keep it steady and well lit. The app reads it automatically — there is no button to press.
  3. The vehicle is recognised You move to the confirmation screen (next step).
Camera not starting? The camera only works over a secure (https) address. If you see "Camera unavailable", use manual token entry below. If the camera should work but doesn't, check that you allowed camera permission for the site in your browser. See the Help guide for more.

Manual token entry (fallback)

Under the camera there is a Manual token entry box. Every printed QR sheet has a short code beneath the code that starts with FT-.

  1. Type the code exactly For example FT-4c1a… — it is not case sensitive.
  2. Tap "Find" The app looks the vehicle up.
Manual entry is rate-limited To keep it safe, manual look-ups are capped (about 10 tries every few minutes). Normal use never hits the limit — if you do, wait a few minutes and try again, or use the camera.

Vehicle recognised

After a successful scan you see the vehicle's details: plate number, vehicle type, fuel type, its last meter reading, and when it was last fuelled. If the fuel type matches your tank, you'll see a green Eligible / Fuel type matches badge.

  • Tap Continue to record the fill.
  • Tap Scan again if it's the wrong vehicle.
If the fuel type does NOT match You go straight to a red Fuel type mismatch screen — you cannot continue from your tank. This is on purpose. See When a screen blocks you.

Step 5

Recording a fuel issue

This is the main event. The form shows your bound tank at the top, the vehicle's plate, and two big number fields.

  1. Enter "Litres dispensed" The exact amount you put in. Decimals are fine (for example 42.5).
  2. Enter the meter reading The field is labelled for the vehicle: "Odometer (km)", "Hour meter (hrs)", or "Energy meter (kWh)". Enter the current reading in whole units, read off the vehicle's own meter.
  3. Check the summary The card below shows the previous reading, the projected stock after this issue, and whether efficiency will be calculated.
  4. Tap "Submit" The issue is recorded instantly and you get a receipt.
Get the reading right Efficiency (km, hours, or kWh per litre) is worked out from the meter. A wrong reading throws off the numbers and can trigger a false alert — so double-check the meter before you submit.
"Calculates after next fill" The very first time a vehicle is fuelled, there is no previous fill to measure against, so there is no efficiency figure yet. That's expected — it appears from the next fill onward.
Tapped Submit twice? No harm done The app recognises a repeated submission and records the fill once. You will never create a duplicate by double-tapping or retrying after a weak signal.

If you change your mind, tap Cancel and rescan at the bottom to start over with a different vehicle.

Step 6

The receipt

After a successful submit you see "Fuel issue recorded" with a green tick. This confirms the issue is safely in the record.

The receipt shows:

  • Litres dispensed and the plate number.
  • The meter reading you recorded and the tank it came from.
  • Stock after issue — the tank's new balance.
  • Efficiency — km/L, hrs/L, or kWh/L for this fill (hour and energy meters also show the familiar litres-per-unit alongside, e.g. "1.20 hrs/L (0.83 L/hr)"), or "Calculates after next fill" for a first fill.
"Abnormal consumption" banner If this fill's fuel economy is outside the expected range for the vehicle type, the receipt shows an amber Abnormal consumption note. The issue is still recorded — nothing is lost. It just gets flagged so a manager can review it. Common innocent causes: a mistyped meter reading, or the vehicle did unusual work. Nothing for you to fix; just make sure your reading was correct.

Tap Done to return to your home screen, ready for the next vehicle.

Step 7

When a screen blocks you

Sometimes the app stops a fill. This is the system doing its job — protecting the fuel and the records. There are three block screens plus the abnormal-consumption note. Here is exactly what each one means and what to do.

🔴 Fuel type mismatch (hard block)

The vehicle uses a different fuel than your tank — for example a Petrol vehicle at your Diesel tank. The screen is red and there is no way to continue. This protects the engine and the stock.

What to do Do not fuel the vehicle from your tank. Tap Scan another vehicle. The vehicle must be fuelled from a tank of the matching fuel type (a different operator/tank). There is no override — not even an administrator can force a mismatch.

🟠 Meter reading lower than the last record

You entered a meter reading that is lower than the vehicle's last recorded reading. A usage meter — odometer, hour meter, or energy meter — only goes up, so the app blocks it and asks for review. The amber screen shows the last recorded reading and what you attempted.

You have two choices:

  • Go back — if you simply mistyped, return and enter the correct, higher reading.
  • Flag for admin review — if you're sure the reading is right (for example the meter was replaced or genuinely reset), flag it. An administrator will check it.
After you flag it You'll see "Flagged for review". You don't need to resubmit. The fuel issue is recorded automatically once an administrator approves a corrected reading. You can move on to the next vehicle. Only an administrator can approve this — not a supervisor, not a manager, and not you.

🟠 Not enough recorded stock

The ledger shows less fuel in your tank than the amount you're trying to issue. The app won't let stock go negative.

What to do If the physical tank really is low, that's expected — a delivery needs recording. If the tank clearly has fuel, the records are behind: a supervisor or administrator must record the missing delivery or an adjustment first. That is not something you do (see the next section). You can tap Adjust amount to enter a smaller quantity that fits, or Back to home.

Good to know

What is not your job

To keep the fuel records honest, some tasks are deliberately kept away from operators. You issue fuel; other people record fuel coming in and correcting the books. This separation is a control, not a limitation on you.

TaskWho does it
Issue fuel to a vehicleYou (operator)
Record a fuel delivery into a tankSupervisor or Administrator
Record a stock adjustment / correctionSupervisor or Administrator
Approve a meter-reading overrideAdministrator only
Assign or change your tankAdministrator only
Why you can't record deliveries Deliveries add fuel to the books. Keeping that in different hands from issuing fuel means no single person controls both sides of the ledger — it's a standard safeguard. If a delivery is missing, tell your supervisor; they'll enter it.

Common questions, fast

  • Locked out? 5 wrong passwords locks the account for 15 minutes. Wait, or ask an admin to unlock/reset it.
  • Forgot your password? An administrator resets it and gives you a temporary one; you set your own at next sign-in.
  • Camera won't start? Use manual token entry (the FT-… code under the QR).
  • "No tank assigned"? An admin must bind a tank to you; sign out and in after they do.
  • Flagged a meter reading? Don't resubmit — it records itself once an admin approves.

For anything else, see the Help / Troubleshooting / FAQ guide or ask your administrator.
