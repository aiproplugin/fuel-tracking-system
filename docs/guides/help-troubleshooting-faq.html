Something not working? Start here

Common problems and exactly how to fix them. Each answer notes who it's for: Operator Admin Everyone.

The 30-second checklist
  • Can't sign in? → wrong password, or the account is locked (5 wrong tries = 15 min).
  • Camera won't start? → use manual token entry (FT-… under the QR).
  • "No tank assigned"? → an admin must bind your tank; then sign out and back in.
  • App blocked your entry? → jump to Blocked entries.

Use the menu on the left to jump to a topic, or read the whole thing top to bottom — it's short. For step-by-step how-tos, see the Operator Guide or the Manager & Admin Guide.

Accounts

Sign-in & passwords

My account is locked Everyone

After 5 wrong passwords the account locks for 15 minutes. Each further wrong try after that roughly doubles the wait (capped at 24 hours).

Fix: wait it out, or ask an administrator to unlock you immediately — on the Users page they tap Unlock on your row (or reset your password, which also unlocks).

I forgot my password Everyone

There is no self-service reset (this is an internal system). An administrator resets it: Users → Reset password → sets a temporary password and gives it to you securely.

Sign in with the temporary password; the app immediately asks you to set your own. A reset also unlocks a locked account.

It keeps saying "Invalid username or password" Everyone

The message is the same whether the username or the password is wrong — that's deliberate, to protect accounts. Check both carefully (watch for caps lock and extra spaces). If you're sure they're right, the account may be locked — see above — or the username may not exist; ask your administrator to confirm it.

The app won't accept my new password Everyone

Passwords must be at least 12 characters and include an upper-case letter, a lower-case letter, a digit, and a symbol (e.g. !, #, @). Also make sure the "new" and "confirm" fields match exactly.

It signed me out right after I changed my password Everyone

That's normal and intended. Changing your password refreshes your session, so the app signs you out and you sign back in with the new password. Nothing is wrong.

I got signed out on my own Everyone

Sessions are short-lived for security (they expire after a period of time). Just sign in again. If it happens constantly, tell your administrator — the server clock or configuration may need a look.

Fuelling

Scanning & camera

The camera won't start / "Camera unavailable" Operator

The browser only allows the camera on a secure address. Work through these:

  1. Check the address bar shows https:// (a padlock), not plain http. If it doesn't, you're on the wrong link — ask your administrator for the correct secure address.
  2. When the browser asks to allow camera access, tap Allow. If you blocked it before, open the site's permissions and re-enable the camera, then reload.
  3. Make sure no other app is using the camera, and that the lens isn't covered.
  4. Still stuck? Use manual token entry — see below.
How do I use manual token entry? Operator

Under the camera there's a Manual token entry box. Every printed QR sheet has a short code beneath the QR that starts with FT-. Type it exactly (it isn't case-sensitive) and tap Find.

"Vehicle not recognized" after scanning Everyone

The token was read but doesn't match an active vehicle. Usual causes:

  • The sticker's token was rotated or deactivated — the old sheet no longer works. An admin should reprint the current sheet from QR Tokens → Print.
  • The vehicle is deactivated in the register.
  • A typo in manual entry — re-check the FT-… code.

Admins: confirm the vehicle has an active token on the QR Tokens page.

Manual entry says I've tried too many times Operator

Manual look-ups are rate-limited to keep the system safe (roughly 10 tries per few minutes; scanning and submitting have their own generous limits too). Normal work never hits this. Wait a few minutes and try again, or use the camera. If you're hitting it a lot, the printed code may be wrong — ask an admin to reprint it.

Tanks & stock

Tanks & stock

"No tank assigned" / the tank I need isn't offered Operator

You're bound to exactly one tank, and only an administrator sets it — you never choose a tank yourself. If you see "No tank assigned", or you're at the wrong tank:

  1. Ask an administrator to assign (or change) your tank: Users → your row → Assign tank.
  2. The change takes effect at your next sign-in — so sign out and back in after they've done it.

You cannot be offered a choice of tanks by design; one operator = one tank per shift.

"Not enough recorded stock" but the tank has fuel Everyone

The ledger shows less than you're issuing, so the app won't let stock go negative. If the physical tank really does have fuel, a delivery or correction hasn't been recorded yet.

Operator: tell your supervisor; you can issue a smaller amount that fits in the meantime, or come back after it's recorded.

Supervisor/Admin: record the missing Delivery, or an Adjustment if there was no delivery, then the issue will go through.

A "Low-stock tanks" alert is showing Admin

One or more tanks dropped below their low-stock threshold. It's a heads-up to schedule a delivery — not an error. The count clears once stock is topped up and the delivery is recorded.

The dashboard shows a reconciliation mismatch Admin

Two very different situations:

  • Cache mismatch (ledger intact): the cached stock number drifted. Safe to repair — on the server run npm run reconcile -- --repair (it's audited).
  • Chain broken: the ledger rows themselves don't add up. This is never auto-repaired — treat it as an incident and investigate what happened before changing anything.

The person who runs the server can produce a per-tank breakdown with npm run reconcile.

When the app stops you

Blocked entries

These blocks are the system protecting the fuel and the records. Here's what each means.

Red "Fuel type mismatch" screen Operator

The vehicle uses a different fuel than your tank (e.g. a petrol vehicle at a diesel tank). This is a hard block with no override — not even an admin can force it, because it protects the engine and the stock.

Fix: don't fuel it from your tank. Tap Scan another vehicle. The vehicle must be filled from a tank of the matching fuel type.

Amber "Meter reading lower than previous record" Operator

Your meter entry is below the vehicle's last recorded reading. A usage meter — odometer (km), hour meter (hrs), or energy meter (kWh) — only goes up, so the app blocks it.

  • If you mistyped: tap Go back and enter the correct, higher reading.
  • If the reading is genuinely right (meter replaced/reset): tap Flag for admin review. You'll see "Flagged for review" — you don't resubmit. The issue records itself once an administrator approves a corrected reading.
I flagged a meter reading — now what? Everyone

Operator: nothing more to do. Move to the next vehicle; the fill is recorded automatically after approval.

Admin: it appears in the exception queue on the dashboard and on the Fuel Issues page. Verify the true reading, then approve with a corrected reading and a reason, or reject with a reason. Only admins can do this.

Why can't a supervisor or manager override the meter reading? Everyone

Overrides change a recorded reading, so the authority is deliberately narrow — administrators only. Supervisors and managers cannot override; operators can only flag for review. Every override is audit-logged with a reason.

Flags & reviews

Alerts & exceptions

An "Abnormal consumption" alert appeared Everyone

The fill's efficiency (km/L, hrs/L, or kWh/L depending on the vehicle) fell outside the expected range for that vehicle type. The fuel issue is still recorded — the alert just marks it for review.

Operator: nothing to fix; just confirm you entered the meter reading correctly. A mistyped reading is the most common innocent cause.

Admin: review it on the dashboard/reports. If lots of clean fills are being flagged, widen the type's efficiency band in Settings; if real waste slips through, tighten it.

Where do I set the expected efficiency range? Admin

Settings (admin only) → edit a vehicle type → pick its meter type (odometer km / hour meter hrs / energy meter kWh) and set the Min and Max band in that unit per litre (min must be below max) → Save band. Changes are audit-logged with before/after values. Any fill outside the band is flagged as abnormal. The meter type locks once vehicles of the type have recorded fills.

"Meter exceptions" KPI is red Admin

Operators have flagged meter readings waiting for your approval. Open the exception queue on the dashboard (or the Fuel Issues page) and clear them — each flagged fill stays unrecorded until you approve a corrected reading or reject it.

An efficiency figure says "Calculates after next fill" Everyone

The first time a vehicle is fuelled there's no previous fill to measure against, so there's no efficiency figure yet. This is expected — it appears from the next fill onward. It is never an error.

Roles & visibility

Access & menus

A menu item I need isn't showing Admin

Menus are role-gated. If you can't see something, your role doesn't include it:

ItemMinimum role
Dashboard, Fuel Issues, Vehicles, Tanks, ReportsSupervisor
Deliveries, Adjustments (recording)Supervisor (Manager = read-only)
Audit trailManager
Users, QR Tokens, Settings, meter overridesAdmin

If you believe your role is wrong, an administrator can change it on the Users page.

Why can managers see deliveries but not record them? Everyone

By design. Recording fuel in (deliveries) and correcting the books (adjustments) is a supervisor/admin control. Managers get full visibility and reporting but are read-only for those two, so no single role controls every side of the ledger.

Operators can't record deliveries at all — is that a bug? Everyone

No — it's intentional. Operators only issue fuel. Deliveries add stock on paper, so they're kept in separate hands. If an operator hits "Not enough stock", a supervisor records the delivery.

How do I add a new operator and get them fuelling? Admin

On Users: Add user (role Operator) → give them the temporary password securely → Assign tank to bind their tank. They sign in, set their own password, sign out/in once so the tank binds, and they're ready. Make sure their tank's vehicles have printed QR stickers (QR Tokens → Print).

Reference

Glossary

TermWhat it means
Fuel issueA recorded fill: litres dispensed to a vehicle from a tank, with a meter reading (km, hrs, or kWh per the vehicle type).
DeliveryFuel received into a tank. Recorded by supervisors/admins. Increases stock.
AdjustmentA signed stock correction with a mandatory reason (e.g. after a physical count).
LedgerThe permanent, append-only record. Every issue, delivery, and adjustment is one row that stores the balance after it.
ReconciliationChecking that each tank's quick "current stock" number still matches the ledger.
Cache mismatchThe current-stock number drifted from the ledger. Safely repairable.
Chain brokenThe ledger rows themselves don't add up. Serious — investigate, never auto-repair.
Meter overrideAdmin approval of a corrected meter reading after an operator flagged a low reading. Reason required, audited.
Abnormal consumptionA fill whose efficiency (km/L, hrs/L, kWh/L) is outside the vehicle type's expected band. Recorded, then flagged for review.
QR tokenAn opaque code (FT-…) that identifies a vehicle when scanned — never the plate. Can be rotated.
Tank bindingThe single tank an operator is tied to. Set by an admin; applies at next sign-in.
Audit trailThe append-only log of who did what, when — logins, overrides, adjustments, and more.
